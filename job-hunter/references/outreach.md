# Recruiter outreach and follow-up

## Cold outreach

Contact a person only when there is a credible relationship to the role. Prefer
the recruiter named on the listing, the relevant hiring manager, or a public
recruiting address.

Write 80–150 words:

1. specific role and reason for contacting them;
2. one or two verified, relevant proof points;
3. concise fit with the team's problem;
4. low-pressure call to action;
5. portfolio/resume link or attachment.

Avoid generic praise, autobiography, urgency tricks, tracking pixels, and
unverifiable performance claims.

## Follow-up cadence

Use the listing or recruiter's stated process first. Otherwise:

- first follow-up: 5–7 business days after application or outreach;
- second and final follow-up: 7–10 business days later;
- stop after a reply, rejection, closed role, or two unanswered follow-ups.

Never schedule a blind send. A scheduled run must re-read the thread and listing
before sending. If no mail connector can re-read the thread, create a draft and
request review.

## Replies

Classify the latest inbound message:

- acknowledgement: update ledger; usually no reply;
- factual question: answer from verified evidence;
- screening or interview: propose times but do not commit unavailable time;
- compensation: summarize the range and request the user's decision;
- rejection: close the pipeline and optionally write a polite response;
- offer or contract: stop for human review.

Record the date, sender role, classification, next action, and due date without
copying private message bodies into a public repository.
